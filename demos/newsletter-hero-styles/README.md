# Newsletter Hero Styles

One newsletter hero ("Dispatch") with byte-identical copy, rendered across design
styles from the ui-ux-pro-max 84-style database (`ui-ux-pro-max/data/styles.csv`).
Built for a blog series about design styles for newsletter writers.

- `gallery.html` — index hub with style-accurate mini-previews of every hero
- `hero-NN-<slug>.html` — one self-contained hero per style (no external assets)

Canonical copy (verified identical across all heroes, MD5 `bdfc6df817`):
wordmark "Dispatch" · kicker "The Weekly Dispatch · Issue N°142" ·
headline "The ideas worth your inbox." · subhead (Friday email, 24,000 readers) ·
email capture + "Subscribe" · "Free forever. Unsubscribe anytime." ·
stats 24,000+/Weekly/5 min/100%.
